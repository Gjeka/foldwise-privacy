Foldwise privacy policy

Effective 20 November 2026.

Foldwise is made by KGWorks. It counts how often you unfold your foldable phone. Everything it records stays on your phone. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and Foldwise never sends your data to us or to anyone else.

What Foldwise records

To count unfolds and show your statistics, Foldwise keeps these on your phone:

  • Fold events. For each time your phone opens, closes or settles half-open: the time, your time zone's offset from UTC at that moment, what happened (unfold, fold, half-open or open), the hinge angle when the phone reports one, and how it was detected (the Android hinge sensor, an iOS Shortcuts automation, or the app itself on iOS).
  • Your settings. When your day starts (midnight unless you change it), your phone model and its rated number of folds, if you enter them.
  • A small summary for the widget. Today's count, your all-time total and the last 7 days, so the home-screen widget can show them without opening the full history. On iPhone, if you install Foldwise's Apple Watch app, your iPhone sends this summary (not your history) to your paired watch, which keeps it for its complications.

Foldwise doesn't record your location, contacts, photos, messages, what you do in other apps, or anything that identifies you.

Where it goes

Nowhere. Foldwise has no servers and doesn't connect to the internet: the Android app doesn't even ask for internet access. We, the developers, can't see your data.

Your phone's own systems can copy it in these cases, which Foldwise doesn't control:

  • iPhone: if you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer, your Foldwise history is included in that backup, like other apps' data. It's protected by Apple's backup encryption and your Apple Account, and only you can restore it.
  • Android: Foldwise opts out of Google's cloud backup. If you move to a new phone with Android's own transfer tool (cable or wireless), your history may be copied to the new phone.
  • Apple Watch: the summary goes from your iPhone to your own paired watch through Apple's watch connection, as for any watch app. It never passes through us.
  • Export: if you use "Export CSV", Foldwise writes a file with your fold events wherever you choose to save or share it. What happens to that file is up to you.

Notifications

On Android, Foldwise shows a quiet, ongoing notification while it counts, with today's count. It's created on your phone; nothing is sent to deliver it.

Deleting your data

  • Settings → Reset all data deletes your whole counting history from the phone. It can't be undone.
  • Uninstalling Foldwise deletes everything it stored, including the widget's summary. Removing the watch app deletes the summary on the watch.

Copies in your phone's backups are deleted the way you delete those backups.

Children

Foldwise is suitable for everyone and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. Because Foldwise collects nothing, a change could only be about what stays on your phone.
