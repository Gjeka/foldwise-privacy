Foldwise support

Foldwise counts how often you unfold your foldable phone, with widgets and statistics that stay on your phone. It's made by KGWorks.

Contact

Questions, problems or ideas: write to askkgworks@gmail.com. Say which phone you have and what you expected to see.

iPhone: counting while the app is closed

iOS doesn't let apps watch the hinge in the background, so Foldwise counts through two automations in the Shortcuts app. The app's guide walks you through them; here are the same steps.

  1. Open Shortcuts and tap + at the bottom right.
  2. Tap Edit at the top right.
  3. In the search field, type Automation. Under Display, tap Opened or Closed.
  4. At the top, tap Opened or Closed and choose Opened.
  5. Search for Log Unfold and tap it to add it.
  6. Leave Shortcuts. The automation saves and turns on by itself.

Then make a second one the same way, choosing Closed and adding Log Fold.

Don't leave either automation on Opened or Closed: then both run every time, and Foldwise can't tell an unfold from a fold, so it counts neither. Settings → Shortcuts automations shows whether both are working.

Android: counting stopped

Android can close apps in the background to save battery, and unfolds then go uncounted. Foldwise warns you on the Today screen when that happens.

  • Allow background use: Settings → Apps → Foldwise → Battery, then choose Unrestricted (or Don't optimise).
  • Some phone makers add their own limits. Foldwise's setup shows the steps for your phone, for example turning on Autostart on Xiaomi.
  • Check that Count unfolds is on in Foldwise's Settings: when it's off, counting is paused.

Your data

  • Export: Settings → Export CSV saves your whole history as a spreadsheet file.
  • Delete: Settings → Reset all data deletes your history. Uninstalling Foldwise deletes everything it stored.
  • Foldwise has no account and sends nothing anywhere: see the privacy policy.
